1. Ingestion of HigherLogic / IBM Community Pages:
   - Ingest tool `ingest.py` looks for standard `<article>` or `<main>` tags. On complex community forum platforms (HigherLogic), content is wrapped in custom CMS divs, causing `_HTMLExtractor` to miss main blocks if the word count heuristic is not reached.
   - Recommendation: Add fallback support for HigherLogic/community DOM containers (`div[id*="Content"]`, `div[class*="blog-content"]`) or allow markdown fallback extraction.
